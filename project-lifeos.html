What It Does

LifeOS is a modular productivity cockpit for choosing priorities, protecting focus, planning days, capturing thoughts, and organizing life into spaces.

Why It Matters

It turns scattered work into visible priorities, focus sessions, calendar structure, and searchable personal context.

Build Notes

  • Now, Today, Spaces, Tasks, Calendar, Notes, Brain, Knowledge, and Resources views.
  • Focus mode with session tracking, break prompts, floating resume dock, and keyboard shortcuts.
  • Firebase-backed persistence with local/offline-friendly behavior.
  • Tauri configuration for native macOS packaging.
