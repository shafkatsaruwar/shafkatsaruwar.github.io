About Me

I build software like someone who has actually had to support the thing after launch. That means I care about boring magic: fewer clicks, cleaner handoffs, useful dashboards, scripts that do the annoying part, and tools that do not collapse the second a real human uses them.

My background is IT support, infrastructure, automation, and production troubleshooting. Translation: I have seen the chaos up close. The mystery spreadsheet. The repeated manual task. The process everyone hates but nobody has time to fix. I am the person who looks at that and goes, yeah, this should be software.

I graduated from UMass Boston with a B.S. in Information Technology, and now I build across web apps, scripts, dashboards, and backend-friendly tooling. I like the work where operator brain meets developer hands.
